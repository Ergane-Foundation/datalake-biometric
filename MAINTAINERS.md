## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests; every change needs a maintainer's approval and
  green CI.
- Triage issues and label them.
- Decide on releases and on changes to the public API, the native bridge, the
  models and the security design.
- Handle security reports privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and
  enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How decisions are made

Small changes are decided in the pull request. Larger changes (new features,
breaking API changes, a different model) start as an issue or discussion so
others can comment; the lead maintainer makes the final call and records the
reason in the issue.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who make several solid pull requests and help others in reviews and
discussions may be invited to become maintainers.
